## Public revenue

Government revenue can come from taxes, fees, profits from public enterprises, grants, borrowing, and other sources. Each source has distributional and incentive effects.

## Public expenditure

Expenditure may be current or capital, transfer-based or purchase-based, and directed toward administration, services, infrastructure, protection, or development.

## Macroeconomic role

Government finance can provide public goods, redistribute income, stabilise demand, and influence productive capacity. Financing choices affect private decisions.

## Quick check

Distinguish a transfer payment from a government purchase of a newly produced good or service.
