## Saving and wealth

Saving is a flow of income not spent during a period. Wealth is a stock of accumulated assets minus liabilities measured at a point in time.

## Sources of saving

Households save through deposits, securities, businesses, and other assets. Firms retain earnings and governments may save when revenue exceeds relevant expenditure.

## Investment connection

In a closed economy, planned saving and investment are linked through income accounting and financial markets. In an open economy, foreign borrowing or lending can bridge the gap.
