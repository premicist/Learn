## Indifference curves

An indifference curve shows combinations of two goods that provide the same preference ranking. Its slope reflects the marginal rate of substitution between goods.

## Budget line

A budget line shows bundles affordable at given income and prices. Its intercepts and slope change when income or prices change.

## Equilibrium

In a standard interior solution, the highest attainable indifference curve touches the budget line where the willingness to substitute matches the relative price.
