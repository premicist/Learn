## Marginal efficiency of capital

The marginal efficiency of capital compares the expected yield from an additional capital asset with its supply price. Investment is more attractive when expected returns exceed financing cost.

## Accelerator principle

The accelerator links desired capital stock to output or sales. Rapid changes in demand can therefore produce larger changes in investment, although adjustment is limited by capacity and expectations.

## Neoclassical and Tobin’s q

Neoclassical investment weighs the desired capital stock against user cost. Tobin’s q compares the market value of installed capital with its replacement cost as a signal for investment incentives.
