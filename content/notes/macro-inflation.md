## Meaning and measurement

Inflation is a sustained rise in the general price level. Price indexes such as the consumer price index track selected baskets and are used to estimate the rate of change.

## Demand-pull and cost-push

Demand-pull inflation can arise when aggregate demand grows faster than productive capacity. Cost-push inflation can arise from higher wages, energy, imported inputs, taxes, or supply disruptions.

## Effects

Inflation changes purchasing power, redistributes between borrowers and lenders, complicates planning, and can reduce confidence when it is high or unpredictable.
