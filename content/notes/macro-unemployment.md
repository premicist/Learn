## Types

Frictional unemployment occurs during job search, structural unemployment reflects changing skills or locations, cyclical unemployment follows weak demand, and seasonal unemployment follows regular patterns.

## Measurement

The unemployment rate compares people without work who are actively seeking work with the labour force. Participation and underemployment provide additional information.

## Costs and natural rate

Unemployment can reduce output, income, skills, wellbeing, and public revenue. The natural rate reflects normal search and structural conditions rather than zero unemployment.
