## Oligopoly

An oligopoly has a small number of significant firms. Each firm considers how rivals may respond to price, output, promotion, investment, and product decisions.

## Cartels

A cartel coordinates output or price to raise joint profit, but members may have incentives to secretly expand sales. Monitoring and enforcement affect stability.

## Price leadership and kinked demand

A price leader may set a price that others follow. A kinked-demand model highlights the possibility that rivals respond differently to price increases and decreases, creating price rigidity.
