## Short-run curves

Short-run analysis includes at least one fixed input. Average fixed cost usually falls with output, while average variable, average total, and marginal cost reflect productivity and input use.

## Marginal relationship

When marginal cost is below an average cost, the average falls; when marginal cost is above it, the average rises. Marginal cost crosses an average curve at its minimum.

## Long-run planning

The long-run average-cost curve traces the lowest unit cost available when the firm can vary plant size and all inputs.
