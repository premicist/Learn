## Meaning of aggregate demand

Aggregate demand is planned expenditure on domestically produced final goods and services at different price levels. It combines private, public, and external demand.

## From model equilibrium

Changes in the price level alter real money balances, interest rates, expenditure, and net exports. The resulting equilibrium combinations trace an aggregate-demand relationship.

## Shifts in aggregate demand

Fiscal policy, monetary conditions, confidence, wealth, foreign income, exchange rates, and investment expectations can shift aggregate demand.
