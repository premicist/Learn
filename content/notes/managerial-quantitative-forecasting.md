## Time series

Time-series analysis uses past observations to identify trend, seasonality, cycles, and irregular variation. It is useful when historical patterns are reasonably stable.

## Smoothing

Moving averages and exponential smoothing reduce noise and highlight underlying patterns. The choice of window or smoothing weight affects responsiveness.

## Regression and indicators

Regression relates demand to explanatory variables such as price, income, and promotion. Barometric methods use leading, coincident, or lagging indicators to anticipate changes.
