## Model setting

The Mundell–Fleming model extends short-run income and interest analysis to a small open economy with capital flows and an exchange rate.

## Flexible exchange rates

With a flexible exchange rate, monetary expansion can affect output through interest rates and currency depreciation. Fiscal expansion may be partly offset by appreciation and weaker net exports.

## Fixed exchange rates

Maintaining a fixed rate requires intervention. This can change the domestic money supply and alter the relative effectiveness of fiscal and monetary policy.
