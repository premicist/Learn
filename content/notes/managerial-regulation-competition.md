## Competition policy

Antitrust policy addresses conduct or structures that may reduce competition, such as collusion, exclusion, abuse of dominance, or harmful mergers.

## Utility regulation

Natural-monopoly conditions may justify price, service, quality, or access regulation when one network can serve the market at lower cost than several parallel networks.

## Patents and operating controls

Patent rights can encourage innovation by granting temporary exclusivity. Operating controls, licensing, and disclosure rules can address safety, quality, and entry concerns.
