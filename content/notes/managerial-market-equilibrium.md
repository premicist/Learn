## Equilibrium

Market equilibrium occurs where quantity demanded equals quantity supplied. It can be found from schedules, equations, or the intersection of curves.

## Adjustment

A shortage can create upward price pressure, while a surplus can create downward pressure. The speed of adjustment depends on contracts, inventories, information, and market institutions.

## Shocks

Demand, supply, policy, technology, expectations, and external events can shift equilibrium. Managers should distinguish a temporary movement from a persistent structural change.
