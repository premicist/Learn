## Risk and uncertainty

Risk refers to situations where possible outcomes and probabilities can be estimated. Uncertainty describes situations where probabilities or outcomes are less known.

## Business exposure

Demand, costs, exchange rates, technology, regulation, accidents, and financing create exposures. Managers identify probability, severity, timing, and ability to respond.

## Risk responses

Diversification, insurance, contracts, reserves, information, flexible capacity, and staged investment can reduce or redistribute risk. Each response has a cost.
