## Demand

Demand is the quantity customers are willing and able to buy at alternative prices during a specified period. Income, tastes, expectations, related goods, and customer numbers can shift demand.

## Supply

Supply is the quantity sellers are willing and able to offer. Input prices, technology, taxes, capacity, expectations, and the number of sellers influence supply.

## Functions and shifts

A function states how quantity depends on price and other variables. A movement along a curve differs from a shift caused by a non-price determinant.
