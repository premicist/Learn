## Environmental policy

Pollution control can use taxes, standards, permits, liability, monitoring, clean-technology support, and information. The best instrument depends on measurement and enforcement capacity.

## Regulatory trade-offs

Regulation can correct market failure but may impose compliance costs, reduce flexibility, create unintended effects, or protect incumbents when poorly designed.

## Public choice

Public-choice analysis studies how voters, officials, agencies, firms, and interest groups shape policy. Government failure can arise from information limits, incentives, rent-seeking, and weak accountability.
