## Peak-load pricing

Peak-load pricing charges more during periods when demand or capacity pressure is high. It can allocate scarce capacity and encourage some users to shift consumption.

## Capacity and demand

The logic is especially relevant when capacity cannot be expanded quickly and the cost of serving an extra user differs between peak and off-peak periods.

## Two-part tariff

A two-part tariff combines a fixed access or membership fee with a per-unit price. It can recover fixed costs while allowing usage to be priced separately.

## Design questions

A firm must consider customer heterogeneity, participation, usage, fairness, and the risk that the fixed fee discourages entry.
