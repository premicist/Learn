## Sales revenue

A sales-revenue objective focuses on total receipts rather than profit alone. Managers may pursue revenue to build market presence, negotiate finance, or support career and organisational goals.

## Growth

Growth can refer to output, sales, assets, market share, employees, or geographic reach. Growth requires finance and can create coordination, risk, and control challenges.

## Managerial discretion

When ownership and management are separated, managers may have room to pursue preferred objectives within minimum profit or survival constraints. Governance shapes that discretion.
