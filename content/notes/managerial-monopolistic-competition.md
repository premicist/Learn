## Differentiation

Firms may differentiate through design, location, service, quality, branding, convenience, or customer experience. Differentiation gives some control over price.

## Short run and long run

A firm can earn profit or incur loss in the short run. Entry of new varieties can reduce the demand faced by existing firms over time.

## Selling cost

Advertising and promotion can inform, persuade, or strengthen a brand. Their value depends on incremental demand, contribution, retention, and competitive response.
