## Saving function

The saving function records planned saving at different income levels. It is closely related to the consumption function because disposable income is allocated between consumption and saving.

## Determinants

Interest rates, wealth, expectations, taxes, credit access, precautionary motives, and demographic factors can change saving behaviour.

## Paradox of thrift

If everyone tries to save more at the same time, consumption may fall. Lower aggregate demand can reduce income, so total realised saving may not increase as much as planned saving.
