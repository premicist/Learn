## Incremental cost

Incremental cost is the additional cost caused by a decision. It may differ from average cost when fixed costs are already committed or when capacity is available.

## Incremental pricing

A short-run price decision can compare incremental revenue with incremental cost, especially for a special order or temporary capacity choice. The firm should also consider opportunity cost and long-run effects.

## Transfer pricing

Transfer pricing sets the value of goods or services exchanged between divisions of the same organisation. It can support performance evaluation, coordination, and resource allocation.

## Balancing objectives

A transfer price that helps one division may distort another division’s incentives. Negotiated, market-based, and cost-based approaches each have advantages and limitations.
