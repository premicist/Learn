## Isoquants

An isoquant shows combinations of two inputs that produce the same output. Its slope reflects the marginal rate of technical substitution.

## Isocosts

An isocost line shows input combinations that have the same total cost. Its slope depends on the relative prices of the inputs.

## Least-cost combination

For an interior solution, the firm reaches a target output at the lowest cost where the technical trade-off matches the input price ratio. Corner solutions are also possible.
