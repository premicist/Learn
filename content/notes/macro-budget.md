## Budget structure

A budget presents expected revenue, expenditure, financing, and policy priorities for a period. It can be classified by current and capital items or by the balance between receipts and spending.

## Deficits and debt

A deficit occurs when relevant expenditure exceeds revenue. It may be financed through borrowing, asset sales, money creation, grants, or other arrangements, each with different consequences.

## Sustainability

Deficit analysis should consider growth, interest costs, maturity, currency exposure, inflation, productive use of borrowing, and the ability to raise future revenue.
