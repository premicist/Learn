## Short-run curves

Short-run analysis includes fixed and variable inputs. Average fixed cost tends to fall as output rises, while average variable, average total, and marginal cost reflect changing productivity and input use.

## Marginal and average cost

Marginal cost cuts an average-cost curve at its minimum. When marginal cost is below the average, the average falls; when it is above, the average rises.

## Long-run average cost

In the long run, the firm can choose plant size and vary all inputs. The long-run average-cost curve traces the least unit cost available at different output levels.

## Cost and output decisions

Cost curves help identify efficient scale, shutdown conditions, break-even output, and possible profit or loss. The correct curve depends on the time horizon.
