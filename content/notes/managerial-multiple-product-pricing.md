## Product portfolio

A firm selling several products considers demand interactions, shared costs, capacity, brand effects, and customer switching across the portfolio.

## Complements and substitutes

The price of one product can affect demand for another. Complements may be bundled, while substitutes require careful cannibalisation analysis.

## Fixed proportions

When products are produced or consumed in fixed proportions, pricing and output decisions should consider the bundle, joint costs, and the value of each component.
