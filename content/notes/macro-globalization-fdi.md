## Globalisation

Globalisation links economies through trade, finance, technology, migration, supply chains, information, and services. It can expand opportunity while increasing exposure to external shocks.

## Foreign direct investment

Foreign direct investment involves a lasting interest or control in productive activity abroad. It can bring capital, technology, skills, and market access, but may also create policy and distributional concerns.

## Business implications

Firms evaluate exchange-rate risk, logistics, regulations, supplier concentration, labour conditions, and demand conditions when operating across borders.
