## Agency relationship

An agency relationship exists when one party delegates a decision to another. Different information, incentives, and risk preferences can create conflicts between owners, managers, employees, and suppliers.

## Information problems

Monitoring, contracts, performance measures, disclosure, and incentive design can reduce but not eliminate conflicts. Measures that are too narrow may encourage undesirable behaviour.

## Behavioural view

Behavioural theories emphasise routines, bounded rationality, coalitions, aspiration levels, and sequential adjustment rather than assuming one perfectly calculated objective.
