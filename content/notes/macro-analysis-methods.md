## Equilibrium

Macroeconomic equilibrium occurs when planned flows are consistent, such as planned expenditure matching output. Disequilibrium appears when inventories, prices, employment, or other variables adjust.

## Static analysis

Static analysis studies relationships at a point or under fixed conditions. It is useful for building a model before examining how variables change.

## Comparative statics and dynamics

Comparative statics compares two equilibria after a change. Dynamic analysis follows adjustment through time and considers expectations, lags, investment, and feedback.
