## Nepal in the global economy

Nepal participates in the world economy through merchandise trade, services, tourism, labour migration, remittances, investment, and development cooperation. These links create opportunities as well as exposure to external shocks.

## Foreign trade

Exports bring foreign exchange and can support production, jobs, and learning. Imports provide goods, fuel, machinery, technology, and inputs. A persistent trade imbalance can place pressure on foreign-exchange reserves and the balance of payments.

## Foreign employment

Foreign employment can provide income and work experience when domestic opportunities are limited. The quality of jobs, recruitment costs, worker safety, skills recognition, and return opportunities affect the overall outcome.

## Remittances

Remittances can support household consumption, education, health, housing, and financial security. A development-focused analysis should also ask whether remittances are channelled into productive investment and how households are affected when migration income changes.

## Policy questions

Nepal can strengthen the benefits of global integration through skills development, safer migration systems, export diversification, better infrastructure, tourism services, and policies that make domestic enterprise more productive.
