## One-shot and repeated games

A one-shot game has no future interaction. Repeated interaction can support cooperation through reputation, retaliation, learning, and future rewards.

## Sequential decisions

In a multistage game, a later decision depends on earlier actions. Backward reasoning helps analyse credible responses and entry or investment choices.

## Managerial applications

Pricing, capacity, advertising, supplier relationships, product launches, and negotiation can all involve strategic interdependence over time.
