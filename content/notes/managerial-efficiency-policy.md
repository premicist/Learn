## Surplus

Consumer surplus measures the difference between willingness to pay and price paid. Producer surplus measures the difference between price received and the minimum acceptable cost.

## Efficiency

A competitive equilibrium can maximise total surplus under restrictive assumptions. Market power, externalities, information gaps, and transaction costs can change that conclusion.

## Policy effects

Taxes create wedges, subsidies encourage activity, price ceilings can create shortages, and price floors can create surpluses. Managers should consider incidence and compliance.
