## Core assumptions

The classical model generally emphasises flexible wages and prices, competitive markets, rational allocation, and a tendency toward full employment through adjustment.

## Labour and output

Employment is connected to the real wage and the marginal productivity of labour. Firms demand labour for the output it can produce, while households supply labour in response to real rewards and preferences.

## Money and neutrality

In the classical view, money primarily affects nominal values in the long run, while real output and employment are determined by real factors under the model’s assumptions.
