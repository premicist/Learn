## Fintech

Fintech applies technology to payments, lending, investment, insurance, identity, and financial information. It can lower costs and expand reach.

## Digital money

Digital money includes electronic representations of value used for payments. Its design affects privacy, convertibility, settlement, monetary control, and financial stability.

## Decentralised finance

Decentralised finance uses programmable networks and digital assets to provide financial functions without relying entirely on traditional intermediaries. It also introduces technology, governance, liquidity, and consumer risks.
