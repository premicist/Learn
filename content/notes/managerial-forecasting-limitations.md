## Forecast error

A forecast is not a guarantee. Error can arise from random variation, poor data, omitted variables, measurement problems, model misspecification, or unexpected shocks.

## Structural change

A historical relationship may change when competitors, technology, regulation, customer habits, or supply conditions change. Models need monitoring and periodic revision.

## Decision use

Managers should use ranges, scenarios, sensitivity analysis, and error measures rather than presenting one number with false precision.
