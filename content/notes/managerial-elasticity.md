## Types of elasticity

Price elasticity measures response to price, income elasticity measures response to income, cross elasticity measures response to another product’s price, and promotional elasticity measures response to advertising or promotion.

## Revenue link

When demand is elastic, a price reduction can increase total revenue under the simple model. When demand is inelastic, a price increase can increase revenue, subject to competitive and strategic responses.

## Managerial use

Elasticity supports pricing, sales forecasts, product positioning, advertising budgets, tax analysis, and capacity planning. Estimates should identify the market and time period.
