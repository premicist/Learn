## Meaning of supply

Supply is the quantity producers are willing and able to offer for sale at different prices during a specified period. It reflects production capacity, expected returns, and the cost of bringing output to market.

## Supply function

A supply function can include product price, input prices, technology, taxes, subsidies, expectations, the number of sellers, and natural conditions.

## Law of supply

Other things remaining constant, quantity supplied generally rises when price rises and falls when price falls. The relationship is shown by a supply schedule and curve.

## Individual and market supply

Individual supply describes one producer’s offer. Market supply is the horizontal addition of the quantities supplied by all relevant producers at each price.
