## Meaning

Transfer pricing sets the value of goods or services exchanged between divisions or units within an organisation.

## Approaches

Market-based, cost-based, negotiated, and dual-price approaches can be used. Each affects reported performance, tax, incentives, and resource allocation differently.

## Capacity and opportunity cost

When the supplying division has spare capacity, marginal cost may be relevant. When capacity is constrained, the opportunity cost of forgone external contribution matters.
