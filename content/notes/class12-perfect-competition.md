## Features of perfect competition

Perfect competition is a model with many buyers and sellers, a homogeneous product, free entry and exit, and good information. No single firm can influence the market price, so the firm is a price taker.

## Market price and firm revenue

The market determines the price through industry demand and supply. For an individual firm, price, average revenue, and marginal revenue are equal. The firm can sell additional units at the market price, subject to its cost conditions.

## Equilibrium output

A profit-maximising firm chooses the output where marginal revenue equals marginal cost, provided the marginal-cost curve is rising at the intersection. In perfect competition this becomes:

**Price = MR = MC**

The firm’s profit or loss is the difference between total revenue and total cost at the chosen output.

## Short-run decisions

A firm may earn abnormal profit, normal profit, or loss in the short run. It continues producing when revenue covers the relevant variable cost; it may temporarily shut down when price falls below average variable cost.

## Long-run adjustment

Free entry and exit change industry supply. Profits attract new firms, while losses encourage firms to leave. In the long run, the model tends toward normal profit when firms can enter or exit freely.
