## Positive economics

Positive economics describes and explains relationships that can be examined with evidence. A positive claim can be tested, although evidence may still be incomplete or disputed.

## Normative economics

Normative economics evaluates what ought to happen using values or criteria such as fairness, efficiency, stability, or freedom. Normative conclusions should make their value assumptions visible.

## Microeconomics and macroeconomics

Microeconomics focuses on individual agents and markets. Macroeconomics studies economy-wide outcomes such as national income, inflation, unemployment, and growth. The two perspectives are connected.

## Business interpretation

A business report should separate observed facts, predictions, and recommendations. This distinction makes it easier to debate evidence and values without confusing them.
