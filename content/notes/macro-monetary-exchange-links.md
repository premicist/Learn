## Interest differentials

Differences in interest rates can influence saving, borrowing, and cross-border capital movement, although risk, liquidity, expectations, and controls also matter.

## Exchange-rate pressure

A currency may appreciate when demand for it rises relative to supply and depreciate when supply exceeds demand. Financial flows can affect this pressure alongside trade flows.

## Policy tension

Authorities may face trade-offs among domestic monetary goals, exchange-rate stability, and capital mobility. The feasible combination depends on the exchange-rate regime.
