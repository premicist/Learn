## Public goods

Public goods are non-rival and difficult to exclude people from using. This can create free-riding and under-provision through private markets.

## Common-property resources

Common resources are rival but difficult to exclude users from. Individual incentives can produce congestion, depletion, or overuse.

## Management options

Rules, permits, community governance, pricing, monitoring, property rights, and public provision can address collective-action problems.

## Quick check

Classify an open-access fishery and a street-light system as a common resource or public good.
