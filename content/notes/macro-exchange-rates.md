## Exchange rate

An exchange rate is the price of one currency in terms of another. Its interpretation depends on the quotation convention used.

## Demand and supply

Trade, income, interest rates, expectations, investment, tourism, remittances, and policy influence demand and supply for currencies.

## Regimes

A fixed regime stabilises the currency around a chosen value through intervention and reserves. A flexible regime allows more market movement, though authorities may still influence conditions.
