## Basic relationship

The consumption function describes planned consumption as related to disposable income and other influences. A simple form separates autonomous consumption from income-induced consumption.

## Average and marginal propensities

The average propensity to consume is consumption divided by income. The marginal propensity to consume is the change in consumption caused by a change in disposable income.

## Determinants

Wealth, interest rates, expectations, taxes, credit conditions, household confidence, and income distribution can shift consumption beyond the simple income relationship.
