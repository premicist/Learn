## Survey methods

Customer surveys can ask about intentions, preferences, and willingness to buy. Responses may be affected by wording, sampling, recall, and the gap between intention and action.

## Market experiments

Test markets and controlled changes can reveal responses to prices, promotion, packaging, or distribution. Design should isolate the treatment and monitor unintended effects.

## Judgement

Expert panels, salesforce opinions, and structured group methods can add context when historical data are limited. They should record assumptions and disagreement.
