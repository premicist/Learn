## Scarcity

Business resources such as labour, capital, time, materials, and managerial attention are limited relative to possible uses. Every allocation therefore excludes another opportunity.

## Opportunity cost

The opportunity cost of a choice is the value of the best alternative forgone. It can be a lost contribution, a delayed project, unused capacity, or time that could have been used elsewhere.

## Production possibility curve

A production possibility curve shows combinations of outputs that can be produced with available resources and technology. Its slope represents the trade-off between outputs.

## Quick check

Identify the opportunity cost of using a machine for a low-margin product when a high-margin order is available.
