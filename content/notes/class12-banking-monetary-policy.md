## What money does

Money serves as a medium of exchange, a measure of value, a store of value, and a standard of deferred payment. These functions reduce the need for direct barter and make economic calculation easier.

## Commercial banks

Commercial banks accept deposits, provide loans, facilitate payments, and create credit. They must balance profitability with liquidity and safety because depositors may want access to their funds while loans are usually held for longer periods.

## Central banking

A central bank issues currency, acts as banker to the government and commercial banks, manages reserves, oversees payment systems, and supports financial stability. It can also influence credit conditions through monetary policy.

## Monetary policy tools

Common tools include policy interest rates, open-market operations, reserve requirements, and selective credit controls. Expansionary policy aims to support spending and credit, while contractionary policy aims to reduce excessive demand or inflationary pressure.

## Transmission and limits

A policy change affects borrowing costs, asset prices, exchange rates, expectations, and eventually output and prices. The effects may be delayed or weakened when banks are cautious, households are heavily indebted, or supply conditions are changing.
