## Profit measures

Accounting profit subtracts recorded expenses from revenue. Economic profit also subtracts implicit opportunity costs of owner-provided resources and alternatives.

## Optimisation

Optimisation means choosing the feasible action that best meets a stated objective. The objective may be profit, value, service quality, growth, risk reduction, or another measurable goal.

## Constraints

Budgets, technology, regulation, capacity, staffing, contracts, and information limit the choices available. A good recommendation states both the objective and the binding constraint.

## Quick check

Explain why a business can report positive accounting profit but zero or negative economic profit.
