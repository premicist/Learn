## Price discrimination

Price discrimination charges different prices for related units or customer groups when differences are not explained only by cost. It requires market power, segmentation, and limited resale.

## Dynamic and value-based pricing

Dynamic pricing adjusts with demand, capacity, timing, or customer conditions. Value-based pricing starts from willingness to pay and customer benefits rather than cost alone.

## Predatory pricing

Predatory pricing is an allegation that a firm prices below a relevant cost measure to weaken rivals and later recover losses through market power. Evidence and competitive effects matter.
