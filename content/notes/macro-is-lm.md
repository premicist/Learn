## IS schedule

The IS schedule shows combinations of income and interest rates at which planned expenditure equals output. Higher interest rates usually reduce interest-sensitive spending, so equilibrium income is lower.

## LM schedule

The LM schedule shows combinations of income and interest rates at which real money demand equals real money supply. Higher income raises transaction demand and usually requires a higher interest rate for money-market balance.

## General equilibrium

The intersection of IS and LM identifies simultaneous equilibrium in the product and money markets. Fiscal and monetary changes shift the schedules and alter output and interest rates.
