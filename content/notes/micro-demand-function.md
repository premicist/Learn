## Meaning of demand

Demand is the quantity buyers are willing and able to purchase at different prices during a specified period. Desire alone is not enough; purchasing power and willingness must also be present.

## Demand function

A demand function expresses quantity demanded as a relationship between price and other determinants such as income, tastes, expectations, the prices of related goods, and the number of buyers.

## Law of demand

Other things remaining constant, quantity demanded generally falls as price rises and rises as price falls. A demand schedule and curve show this relationship in table and diagram form.

## Types and interpretation

Individual demand concerns one buyer, while market demand combines buyers. Derived demand comes from demand for another product or service, such as demand for labour used to produce output.
