## Marginal thinking

A marginal change is the additional effect of one more unit, hour, customer, worker, project, or action. Managers compare the additional benefit with the additional cost rather than relying only on totals.

## Decision rule

When the marginal benefit of an activity exceeds its marginal cost, expanding it can improve the objective. The best interior choice is often found where marginal benefit and marginal cost are equal.

## Incremental analysis

Incremental analysis compares the consequences of one option with another. It should include relevant opportunity costs, avoid sunk costs, and state the time horizon.
