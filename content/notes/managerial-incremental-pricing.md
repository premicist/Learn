## Incremental cost

Incremental cost is the additional cost caused by a decision. It can be below average cost when fixed costs are already committed and capacity is available.

## Short-run price

For a temporary order, a firm may compare incremental revenue with incremental cost while also considering opportunity cost, quality, customer conflict, and future expectations.

## Long-run caution

Repeated prices below economic cost may be unsustainable. A short-run contribution decision should not be confused with a long-run pricing strategy.
