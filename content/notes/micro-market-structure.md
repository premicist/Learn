## Market structure

Market structure describes the number and size of firms, product differentiation, barriers to entry, information, and the degree of price control. These features shape strategic choices and outcomes.

## Main structures

Perfect competition, monopoly, monopolistic competition, and oligopoly are models that highlight different combinations of competition, market power, and interdependence.

## Profit maximisation

Profit is total revenue minus total economic cost. A standard objective is to choose output where marginal revenue equals marginal cost, provided the relevant second-order and operating conditions are satisfied.

## Beyond profit

Firms may also pursue growth, market share, survival, quality, innovation, or social goals. A clear analysis states which objective is being used.
