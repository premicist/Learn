## Effective demand

Effective demand is the level of aggregate demand at which firms’ expected receipts match the output and employment they choose. It determines the short-run equilibrium in the Keynesian framework.

## Aggregate demand components

Consumption and investment form the core of a simple private economy. Government spending, taxes, exports, and imports extend the model to public and open-economy settings.

## Unemployment

When planned expenditure is too low, firms may reduce production and employment even if workers are willing to work. This is demand-deficient or involuntary unemployment.
