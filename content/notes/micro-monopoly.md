## Monopoly conditions

A monopoly has one dominant seller and barriers that limit entry. The firm faces the market demand curve and therefore must consider the effect of output on price.

## Revenue and equilibrium

Because demand slopes downward, marginal revenue lies below average revenue. The monopolist chooses output where marginal revenue equals marginal cost and then reads price from the demand curve.

## Short run and long run

A monopolist may earn profit or loss in the short run. Strong barriers can allow abnormal profit to persist in the long run, depending on demand and cost.

## Evaluation

Monopoly can support innovation or scale in some situations, but market power may reduce output, raise price, and create deadweight loss. Regulation and competition policy require context.
