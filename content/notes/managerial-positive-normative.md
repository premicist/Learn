## Positive analysis

Positive analysis describes or predicts relationships that can be examined with evidence, such as how a price change may affect sales.

## Normative analysis

Normative analysis recommends what should happen using criteria such as profitability, fairness, efficiency, sustainability, or resilience. The chosen criteria should be stated clearly.

## Decision communication

A strong managerial report separates observed facts, model-based forecasts, assumptions, risks, and recommendations. This makes disagreement more precise and useful.

## Quick check

Classify “sales fell after the price increase” and “the firm should reverse the price increase.”
