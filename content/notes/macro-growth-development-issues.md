## Growth and development

Economic growth is a sustained rise in real output or income. Development is broader and includes health, education, security, capabilities, opportunity, and institutional quality.

## Sources of growth

Capital formation, human skills, technology, infrastructure, institutions, entrepreneurship, and resource allocation can raise productive capacity.

## Poverty and inequality

Poverty concerns inadequate resources or capabilities, while inequality concerns distribution. Growth can reduce poverty without reducing inequality, so both outcomes need separate attention.

## Contemporary issues

Foreign employment, environmental sustainability, digital change, and social protection shape how growth affects households and firms. Policy should consider resilience as well as expansion.
