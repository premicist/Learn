## External effect

An externality occurs when an activity affects people not fully reflected in the decision-maker’s private costs or benefits.

## Negative and positive

Pollution is a negative production externality, while education or vaccination can create positive consumption or production effects.

## Policy responses

Taxes, subsidies, standards, tradable permits, property rights, information, and negotiated solutions can align private incentives with social effects.
