## Oligopoly

An oligopoly has a small number of significant firms. Each firm’s price, output, advertising, and investment decisions can affect the others, so strategic interdependence is central.

## Duopoly

A duopoly is an oligopoly with two main sellers. Simple models show how firms may choose quantities or prices while anticipating the rival’s response.

## Cartels

A cartel is an agreement among firms to coordinate output, price, or market division. A cartel may seek joint monopoly profit but each member can have an incentive to secretly expand sales.

## Stability and evaluation

Cartels are difficult to maintain when members differ, monitoring is weak, demand changes, or new entry is possible. Their effects on consumers, efficiency, and competition should be assessed carefully.
