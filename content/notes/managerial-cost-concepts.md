## Explicit and implicit costs

Explicit costs involve direct payments. Implicit costs are opportunity costs of owned resources such as the owner’s time, building, or capital.

## Fixed and variable costs

Fixed cost does not change with output over a chosen short-run range, while variable cost changes with output. The classification can change over a longer horizon.

## Average and marginal cost

Average cost is cost per unit. Marginal cost is the additional cost of one more unit and helps analyse output, pricing, and capacity decisions.
