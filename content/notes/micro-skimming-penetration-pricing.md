## Price skimming

Price skimming begins with a relatively high price to target buyers with high willingness to pay, then may lower the price as the market broadens or competition increases.

## Penetration pricing

Penetration pricing begins with a low price to attract customers, build volume, discourage entry, or encourage network adoption. It requires enough finance and capacity to sustain the strategy.

## Predatory pricing

Predatory pricing is an allegation that a firm prices below a relevant cost measure to weaken rivals and later use market power. Analysis requires evidence of intent, duration, recoupment, and competitive effect.

## Choosing a strategy

Launch pricing depends on demand, product life cycle, switching costs, competition, capacity, brand, and regulation. There is no single strategy suitable for every market.
