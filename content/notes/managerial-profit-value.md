## Profit maximisation

Profit maximisation chooses output, price, input use, or strategy to maximise the difference between revenue and economic cost under stated conditions.

## Value maximisation

Value maximisation considers the present value of expected future cash flows, risk, investment, reputation, and long-term competitive position.

## Time and risk

A decision that raises current profit may reduce future value if it damages quality, trust, capacity, innovation, or customer relationships.
