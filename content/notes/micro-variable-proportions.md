## Total, average, and marginal product

Total product is total output. Average product is output per unit of the variable input. Marginal product is the additional output from one more unit of that input.

## The law of variable proportions

As more units of a variable input are combined with a fixed input, marginal product may first rise, then fall, and eventually become negative. The pattern depends on technology and capacity.

## Stages of production

The rational operating region is usually associated with positive but diminishing marginal product. A table can show the turning points more clearly than a diagram alone.

## Link with cost

When marginal product rises, the cost of obtaining additional output may fall. When marginal product falls, the marginal cost of output may rise, holding input prices constant.
