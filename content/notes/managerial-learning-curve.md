## Learning effect

A learning curve describes how average time or cost per unit can fall as cumulative experience increases. Improvement may come from practice, process redesign, standardisation, and better coordination.

## Forecasting use

Learning estimates help plan labour requirements, delivery dates, capacity, bids, and expected unit costs. They should distinguish cumulative output from current-period output.

## Limits

Learning can slow or stop when processes mature, staff change, technology changes, or complexity increases. Managers should test assumptions with actual performance.

## Quick check

Give one reason a new production line may learn faster at first but show smaller gains later.
