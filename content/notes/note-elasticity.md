## Meaning of price elasticity

Price elasticity of demand measures the responsiveness of quantity demanded to a change in price. It helps explain whether buyers change their purchases a little or a lot when price changes.

## Measurement and degrees

A common formula is:

**PED = Percentage change in quantity demanded ÷ Percentage change in price**

The value is often written as an absolute number for convenience. Demand is elastic when the value is greater than one, inelastic when it is less than one, and unit elastic when it equals one. Perfectly elastic and perfectly inelastic cases are useful limiting models.

## Factors determining elasticity

Availability of substitutes, whether a good is a necessity or luxury, the share of income it uses, the time available for adjustment, and how narrowly the market is defined can all influence elasticity.

## Elasticity and revenue

When demand is elastic, a price reduction can increase total revenue because quantity responds proportionally more. When demand is inelastic, a price increase can increase total revenue. The relationship should be checked with a table or calculation rather than assumed from price alone.

## Business decision-making

Elasticity can inform pricing, sales forecasts, taxation analysis, product positioning, and inventory planning. A manager should consider the relevant market, time period, customer group, and other conditions before applying an elasticity estimate.

## Quick check

If price rises by 10 percent and quantity demanded falls by 20 percent, calculate the elasticity value and classify demand. Then explain what may happen to total revenue.
