## Rent as a factor reward

Rent is a payment for the use of land or other natural resources. Resource markets allocate scarce factors among competing uses.

## Economic rent

Economic rent is the part of a factor payment above the minimum needed to keep the factor in its current use. Scarcity, quality, location, and alternative uses influence it.

## Demand for resources

Demand for a resource is derived from demand for the goods and services it helps produce. Productivity and product price therefore affect the value of a factor’s service.

## Distribution and policy

Rent can influence income distribution, land use, taxation, and development. A complete answer separates the payment, the source of scarcity, and the policy question.
