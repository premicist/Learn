## Basic flow

In a simple economy, households provide factors of production and receive income. Firms use those factors to produce goods and services that households purchase.

## Adding government

Government enters through taxation, public expenditure, transfers, and borrowing. These flows change disposable income and aggregate demand.

## Adding the external sector

Exports bring spending into the domestic economy, while imports send spending abroad. Saving, taxation, and imports are leakages; investment, government spending, and exports are injections.
