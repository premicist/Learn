## Income elasticity

Income elasticity measures how quantity demanded responds to income. It helps classify goods and anticipate how demand may change as household purchasing power changes.

## Cross elasticity

Cross elasticity measures the response of demand for one good to a change in the price of another. A positive relationship can suggest substitutes, while a negative relationship can suggest complements.

## Advertising elasticity

Advertising elasticity examines the response of demand to promotional expenditure. The result depends on message quality, competition, customer awareness, and time lag.

## Elasticity of supply

Supply elasticity measures how quantity supplied responds to price. Capacity, inventory, input flexibility, time, and technology influence the response.

## Business decisions

Elasticity informs pricing, product positioning, sales forecasts, advertising budgets, and tax analysis. Every estimate should state its market, time period, and customer group.
