## Purpose

Cost estimation helps predict how total, average, and marginal cost may change with output, input prices, technology, and operating conditions.

## Methods

Managers may use accounting data, engineering information, high-low comparisons, regression, and activity-based analysis. Each method depends on data quality and stable definitions.

## Interpretation

Estimated relationships should be checked for range, outliers, seasonality, omitted variables, and changes in capacity. Extrapolation beyond the observed range can be risky.

## Quick check
