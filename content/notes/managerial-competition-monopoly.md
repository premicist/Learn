## Perfect competition

A competitive firm takes market price as given and chooses output where price equals marginal cost under standard operating conditions. Entry and exit influence long-run profit.

## Monopoly

A monopolist faces the market demand curve and chooses output where marginal revenue equals marginal cost, then determines price from demand. Barriers to entry help sustain market power.

## Evaluation

Competition tends to increase output and limit price under the model, while monopoly may reduce output and create deadweight loss. Real markets can include scale, innovation, and regulation considerations.
