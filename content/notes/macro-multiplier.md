## Investment multiplier

An initial increase in autonomous investment raises someone’s income, part of which is spent again. Repeated rounds of spending create a larger total change in equilibrium income.

## Fiscal multipliers

Government expenditure, taxes, and balanced-budget changes affect income through different channels. Tax changes usually have a smaller first-round effect because households may save part of the income change.

## Foreign-trade multiplier

Exports inject spending, while imports create a leakage. The open-economy multiplier is smaller when a larger share of additional income is saved, taxed, or spent on imports.
