## Aggregate supply

Aggregate supply represents the output firms are willing to produce at different price levels, given technology, input costs, expectations, and productive capacity.

## Short run and long run

Short-run supply can respond to demand when wages or other costs adjust slowly. Long-run supply depends more on labour, capital, technology, institutions, and productive capacity.

## Shifts

Supply can shift because of wage changes, input prices, productivity, taxes, regulations, expectations, natural events, or changes in the capital stock.
