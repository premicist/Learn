## Sources of rigidity

Contracts, minimum standards, efficiency wages, morale, bargaining, information costs, and coordination problems can slow wage adjustment.

## Labour supply and money wages

Workers respond to real purchasing power, but decisions also depend on taxes, benefits, working conditions, expectations, and household responsibilities.

## Output effects

If money wages are fixed while prices change, the real wage and firms’ labour demand can change. This can affect employment, costs, and short-run aggregate supply.
