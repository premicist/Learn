## Phases

Expansion involves rising activity, a peak marks a high point, contraction involves falling activity, and a trough marks a low point before recovery.

## Sources

Fluctuations can arise from demand shocks, productivity changes, financial stress, external events, inventory adjustment, policy shifts, and changing confidence.

## Business effects

Cycles influence sales, inventories, employment, financing, investment, defaults, and pricing. Firms often respond through cash planning, flexible capacity, and scenario analysis.
