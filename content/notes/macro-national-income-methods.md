## Product method

The product method sums value added across sectors. Value added is output value minus the value of intermediate inputs, which prevents the same production from being counted more than once.

## Income and expenditure methods

The income method totals wages, rent, interest, profits, and relevant adjustments. The expenditure method totals final consumption, investment, government purchases, and net exports.

## Measurement difficulties

Informal production, unpaid household work, changing quality, illegal activity, price changes, depreciation, and incomplete records can make measurement difficult.
