## Gross and net

Gross measures include production before deducting depreciation. Net measures subtract the loss of productive capital during the period. The distinction matters when assessing sustainable income.

## Domestic and national

Domestic product is based on production within a territory. National product adjusts domestic production for net factor income received from and paid to the rest of the world.

## Income available to households

Personal income concerns income received by people, while disposable income adjusts personal income for personal taxes and transfers. Per-capita income divides an aggregate by population.
