## Stance and instruments

Expansionary fiscal policy raises demand through higher spending or lower taxes. Contractionary policy reduces demand through lower spending or higher taxes.

## Automatic stabilisers

Progressive taxes, unemployment support, and other rules can soften fluctuations without a new discretionary decision each time conditions change.

## Multiplier limits

Leakages, imports, taxes, capacity constraints, financing, interest rates, expectations, and supply responses can make the actual multiplier differ from the simple model.
