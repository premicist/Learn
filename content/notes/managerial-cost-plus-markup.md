## Cost-plus method

Cost-plus pricing adds a margin to an estimated cost base. It is simple and can support cost recovery when demand and cost estimates are reasonably stable.

## Mark-up versus margin

A mark-up percentage is calculated on a cost base, while a margin percentage is calculated on selling price. The base must be stated to avoid confusion.

## Limits

Cost-plus rules can ignore willingness to pay, competition, capacity, customer value, and strategic response. Managers should compare the result with market evidence.

## Quick check

Explain why a 25 percent mark-up on cost is not the same as a 25 percent margin on selling price.
