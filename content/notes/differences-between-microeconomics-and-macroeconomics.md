In 1933, Norwegian economist **Sir Ragnar Frisch** bifurcated economics into Microeconomics and Macroeconomics. Both branches analyze resource allocation at different levels of aggregation.

![micro-vs-macro](/images/uploads/micro-vs-macro.png)

### Interdependence & Economic Paradoxes

* **Complementary Nature:** Micro and macroeconomics are interdependent. Aggregate national demand is the sum of individual household demands.
* **Paradox of Thrift:** What is beneficial at the micro level may be harmful at the macro level. Individual saving increases personal security, but universal aggregate saving reduces total national expenditure, output, and income.
