## Objectives

Monetary policy may pursue price stability, sustainable output, employment, financial stability, and orderly external conditions. Objectives can conflict in the short run.

## Instruments

Policy rates, open-market operations, reserve requirements, standing facilities, communication, and targeted measures can influence liquidity, credit, and expectations.

## Transmission

Policy affects market rates, asset prices, exchange rates, credit supply, expectations, spending, employment, and inflation with uncertain lags.
