## Money and capital markets

Money markets handle relatively short-term funds and liquidity. Capital markets support longer-term financing through instruments such as shares and bonds.

## Financial inclusion

Financial inclusion means people and firms can access useful, affordable, safe, and suitable financial services. Access can support saving, payments, insurance, and productive investment.

## Risks and safeguards

Inclusion must be accompanied by consumer protection, financial literacy, data security, responsible lending, and effective supervision.
