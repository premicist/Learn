## Meaning and scope

Managerial Economics applies economic concepts and analytical tools to decisions made by firms, managers, and other organisations. It focuses on choices about resources, customers, operations, prices, and strategy.

## Decision environment

Managers work with limited information, competing objectives, changing markets, and constraints on time, finance, technology, and capacity. Economic models help make those trade-offs explicit.

## Practical uses

The subject supports demand analysis, forecasting, production planning, cost control, pricing, investment, market strategy, and evaluation of public policy.
