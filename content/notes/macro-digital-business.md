## Digital activity

The digital economy includes data-intensive services, online platforms, digital payments, remote delivery, and technology-enabled production. These activities can change productivity and market reach.

## Measurement challenge

Some digital services are free at the point of use or improve quality without a clear price. National accounts therefore need careful treatment of output, productivity, and consumer benefits.

## Business environment

Digital adoption can lower transaction costs, widen markets, and change labour demand. It can also create adjustment costs, skill gaps, concentration, and new forms of risk.
