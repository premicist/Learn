## The budget constraint

A budget line shows the combinations of two goods a consumer can buy with given income and prices. Its intercepts and slope change when income or prices change.

## Affordable and unaffordable bundles

Bundles on the line use the full budget, bundles inside it are affordable with money left over, and bundles outside it are unaffordable under the current constraint.

## Tangency condition

In the standard interior solution, consumer equilibrium occurs where the highest attainable indifference curve is tangent to the budget line. The marginal rate of substitution equals the relative price ratio at that point.

## Corner solutions

A consumer may choose a corner when preferences or prices make one good preferable at the margin. The tangency condition should not be applied mechanically when an interior solution does not exist.
