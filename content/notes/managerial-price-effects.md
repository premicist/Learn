## Price effect

The total response to a price change combines the substitution effect and the income effect. The direction and size depend on preferences, income, and related prices.

## Substitution effect

When a good becomes relatively cheaper, consumers tend to substitute toward it and away from alternatives, holding satisfaction or purchasing power conceptually constant.

## Income effect

A price change alters real purchasing power. For a normal good, greater real income tends to raise demand; for an inferior good, it may reduce demand.
