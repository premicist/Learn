## Two-sector model

The two-sector model includes households and firms. Equilibrium income occurs where planned consumption plus investment equals output.

## Three-sector model

Adding government introduces public expenditure, taxes, and transfers. Disposable income and aggregate expenditure now depend on fiscal choices.

## Four-sector model

The open-economy model adds exports and imports. Net exports can raise or reduce demand depending on trade conditions and domestic income.
