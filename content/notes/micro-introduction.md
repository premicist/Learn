## Meaning and scope

Microeconomics studies the choices of consumers, firms, and resource owners, together with the markets in which they interact. It examines how scarce resources are allocated among competing uses.

## Microeconomic questions

Typical questions ask how prices are formed, why quantity demanded changes, how firms choose inputs and output, and how market structures affect efficiency and distribution.

## Uses in business

Microeconomic tools support pricing, demand forecasting, production planning, cost control, market analysis, and evaluation of regulation. A useful analysis states the assumptions before applying a model.

## Thinking at the margin

Many decisions compare the additional benefit and additional cost of one more unit or action. Marginal reasoning helps explain why a choice can be sensible even when total costs or benefits are large.
