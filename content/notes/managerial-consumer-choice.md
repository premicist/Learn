## Consumer problem

A consumer chooses among bundles of goods and services subject to income, prices, time, information, and other constraints.

## Preferences

Preferences rank alternatives according to satisfaction or priorities. A consistent ordering helps analyse substitution, trade-offs, and demand responses.

## Managerial relevance

Understanding customer choice supports product design, bundling, positioning, segmentation, and pricing. Actual behaviour may also reflect habits, framing, and bounded rationality.

## Quick check

Identify one constraint and one preference that could affect a customer’s choice of two competing products.
