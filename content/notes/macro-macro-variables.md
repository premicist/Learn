## Key variables

Output, national income, employment, unemployment, inflation, interest rates, money, exchange rates, and the balance of payments are central macroeconomic variables.

## Stocks and flows

A stock is measured at a point in time, such as wealth or the money supply. A flow is measured over a period, such as income, saving, investment, exports, or government expenditure.

## Nominal and real values

Nominal values use current prices. Real values remove the effect of price changes so that quantities and purchasing power can be compared more meaningfully over time.
