## Meaning and scope

Macroeconomics studies the economy as a whole. It brings together households, firms, government, and the external sector to explain aggregate output, employment, prices, income, and growth.

## Main questions

Macroeconomic analysis asks why production and employment rise or fall, how inflation develops, how interest rates affect spending, and how policy can support stability and long-run progress.

## Uses and limits

Macro indicators help businesses plan demand, costs, investment, and finance. They are useful summaries, but averages can hide differences across households, sectors, regions, and firms.
