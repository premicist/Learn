## National output and income

National income accounting records the value of production and the income generated by that production. In principle, the product, income, and expenditure approaches measure the same economic activity from different angles.

## Core aggregates

GDP measures final production within a territory. GNP adjusts for net factor income from abroad. NDP and NNP subtract depreciation, while personal and disposable income describe income received and available for spending.

## Three approaches

The product approach adds value added, the income approach adds factor incomes, and the expenditure approach adds consumption, investment, government spending, and net exports. Avoiding double counting is essential.
