## Contribution

Contribution per unit is price minus variable cost per unit. It contributes toward fixed cost and then profit after fixed cost is covered.

## Break-even

Break-even output is reached when total revenue equals total cost. Under constant-price and constant-variable-cost assumptions, it equals fixed cost divided by contribution per unit.

## Applications

Break-even analysis supports product launch, capacity, sales targets, pricing, and sensitivity analysis. Its assumptions should be stated because costs and prices may change with scale.

## Quick check

What happens to break-even output if fixed cost rises while price and variable cost per unit remain unchanged?
