## Economies of scale

Economies of scale occur when average cost falls as the scale of production increases. Specialisation, bulk purchasing, technology, and spreading fixed costs can contribute.

## Diseconomies of scale

Diseconomies occur when average cost rises as the firm becomes larger. Coordination, communication, congestion, and control problems may become more important.

## Economies of scope

Economies of scope arise when producing two or more products together costs less than producing them separately. Shared facilities, distribution, information, or brand capabilities may help.

## Break-even analysis

Break-even occurs when total revenue equals total cost. With constant price and variable cost assumptions, break-even quantity can be introduced as fixed cost divided by contribution per unit.
