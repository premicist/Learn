## Purpose

The balance of payments records economic transactions between residents and non-residents over a period. It helps analyse external sustainability and financing needs.

## Main accounts

The current account includes goods, services, income, and transfers. Capital and financial accounts record capital transfers and transactions in financial assets and liabilities.

## Interpretation

A current-account deficit is not automatically harmful; its meaning depends on what finances it, whether funds support productive activity, and how external liabilities evolve.

## Quick check

Distinguish an export of services from a financial inflow in the balance of payments.
