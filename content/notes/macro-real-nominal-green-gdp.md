## Nominal and real GDP

Nominal GDP values current production at current prices. Real GDP values production using a reference price structure so that changes in quantities can be separated from inflation.

## GDP deflator and price indexes

The GDP deflator compares nominal GDP with real GDP. Consumer and other price indexes track selected baskets, so each index answers a slightly different measurement question.

## Green GDP

Green GDP attempts to account for environmental depletion and damage alongside measured production. It reminds analysts that output growth and sustainable welfare are not identical.
