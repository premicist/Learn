## Gains from trade

Trade allows specialisation according to relative opportunity costs. Exchange can expand consumption possibilities even when one party is more productive in every activity.

## Business choices

Firms compare domestic and external suppliers using price, quality, reliability, exchange-rate exposure, logistics, regulation, and resilience.

## Policy effects

Tariffs, quotas, standards, subsidies, and trade restrictions change costs, prices, market access, and competitive pressure. The effect may differ across producers and consumers.
