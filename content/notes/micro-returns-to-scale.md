## Meaning of returns to scale

Returns to scale describe how output responds when all inputs are increased in the same proportion over the long run. They are different from diminishing marginal product of one variable input in the short run.

## Increasing returns

Increasing returns occur when output rises more than proportionally. Specialisation, indivisibilities, learning, and network effects may contribute.

## Constant and decreasing returns

Constant returns occur when output rises in the same proportion as inputs. Decreasing returns occur when output rises less than proportionally, perhaps because coordination becomes difficult at a large scale.

## Business relevance

Returns to scale influence plant size, expansion, outsourcing, organisation, and long-run cost. The best scale depends on technology, demand, finance, and managerial capacity.

## Quick check

If all inputs double and output rises from 100 to 230 units, classify the returns to scale and explain why.
