## Keynesian range

With substantial idle capacity and sticky costs, demand changes can raise output and employment with limited immediate price pressure.

## Intermediate range

As capacity constraints become binding, additional demand can raise both output and prices. The balance changes as bottlenecks emerge.

## Classical range

When resources are fully employed and prices adjust, demand expansion may mainly raise the price level rather than sustainable real output.
