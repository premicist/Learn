## Peak load

Peak-load pricing charges more when demand or capacity pressure is high. It can allocate scarce capacity and encourage customers to shift usage.

## Two-part tariff

A two-part tariff combines a fixed access fee with a per-unit charge. It can recover fixed costs while retaining a usage price.

## Design issues

Managers consider customer heterogeneity, participation, demand, fairness, capacity, and the risk that an access fee excludes valuable users.
