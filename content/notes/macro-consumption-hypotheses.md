## Absolute income

The absolute-income view links current consumption to current disposable income, usually with consumption rising less than proportionally as income increases.

## Relative income

The relative-income view suggests that spending can depend on social comparison and past consumption habits, not only on a person’s current income.

## Life-cycle and permanent income

Life-cycle reasoning connects spending and saving with expected lifetime resources. Permanent-income reasoning separates predictable long-term resources from temporary income changes.
