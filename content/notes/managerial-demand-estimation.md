## Estimation purpose

Demand estimation identifies how sales relate to price, income, promotion, competitors, seasonality, distribution, and customer characteristics.

## Research design

A useful estimate defines the product, market, customer group, time period, unit of measurement, and variables before collecting data.

## Managerial use

Estimates guide price setting, capacity, inventory, promotion, product launch, and resource allocation. Results should be treated as evidence with uncertainty rather than exact truth.
