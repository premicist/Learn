## The firm

A firm combines resources, technology, information, and authority to produce goods or services. Its decisions are shaped by ownership, governance, competition, finance, and uncertainty.

## Possible objectives

Profit, value, sales revenue, market share, growth, survival, quality, innovation, and social performance can all matter. The relevant objective depends on ownership and strategy.

## Objective conflicts

Higher sales may require lower prices or higher promotion, while rapid growth may reduce current profit. Managers should identify how objectives interact over time.
