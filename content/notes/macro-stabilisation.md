## Demand management

Fiscal and monetary policies can influence aggregate demand. Their timing, size, financing, and credibility affect effectiveness and side effects.

## Supply-side responses

Productivity improvement, better infrastructure, skills, competition, and reliable institutions can expand productive capacity and reduce cost pressure over time.

## Trade-offs

Stabilisation can involve trade-offs among output, employment, inflation, debt, exchange-rate pressure, and distribution. A suitable response depends on the shock.
