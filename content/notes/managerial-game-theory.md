## Strategic setting

A strategic decision is one where the payoff from an action depends on what another decision-maker does. A payoff matrix lists choices and outcomes for each combination.

## Nash equilibrium

A Nash equilibrium is a set of choices where no player can improve its payoff by changing alone, given the other player’s choice. It need not be socially efficient.

## Prisoner’s dilemma

The prisoner’s dilemma shows how individually rational choices can produce a worse joint outcome than cooperation when trust and enforcement are limited.
