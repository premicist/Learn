## Product differentiation

Monopolistic competition has many sellers but differentiated products. Branding, design, location, service, and perceived quality can give each firm some price control.

## Short-run equilibrium

The firm faces a downward-sloping demand curve for its differentiated product and chooses output using revenue and cost conditions. It may earn profit or experience loss in the short run.

## Long-run adjustment

Entry of new varieties can reduce the demand faced by existing firms. In the standard model, long-run economic profit tends toward normal profit, although excess capacity may remain.

## Selling cost and advertising

Selling cost aims to shift or make demand more responsive. Advertising can inform, persuade, or build a brand. The optimal level depends on incremental revenue, incremental cost, and strategic response.
