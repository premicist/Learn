## Short-run relationship

The short-run Phillips curve presents a possible inverse relationship between inflation and unemployment under particular expectations and supply conditions.

## Expectations

With adaptive expectations, people update beliefs from past inflation. With rational expectations, people use available information about policy and the economy, changing how policy affects outcomes.

## Long run

The long-run relationship is often drawn as vertical at the natural rate because attempts to keep unemployment below it can lead to accelerating inflation rather than lasting employment gains.
