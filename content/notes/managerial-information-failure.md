## Quality uncertainty

When buyers cannot easily observe quality, high-quality sellers may struggle to distinguish themselves and low-quality products may drive good products from the market.

## Signals and screening

Warranties, certifications, brands, reviews, deposits, tests, and contracts can signal or screen quality, but they also have costs and can be manipulated.

## Policy and management

Disclosure, standards, liability, audits, and consumer education can improve trust. Firms also use reputation and guarantees to reduce information problems.
