## Commercial banks

Banks accept deposits, make loans, provide payments, transform maturities, and help channel saving toward investment. Their balance sheets connect borrowers and lenders.

## Deposit creation

When banks lend, they can create deposits subject to reserves, capital, regulation, liquidity, borrower demand, and repayment. Lending is not unlimited or automatic.

## Central bank

A central bank supports monetary stability, provides settlement and liquidity services, oversees parts of the financial system, and may act as lender of last resort.
