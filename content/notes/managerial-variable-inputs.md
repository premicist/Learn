## Product measures

Total product is total output, average product is output per unit of a variable input, and marginal product is the additional output from one more unit.

## Variable proportions

With a fixed input, adding more of a variable input may initially raise marginal product, then produce diminishing marginal returns, and eventually create congestion.

## Input choice

A firm compares the value of an input’s marginal product with its price. The relevant decision also depends on output demand, capacity, quality, and coordination.
