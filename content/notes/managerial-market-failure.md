## Meaning

Market failure occurs when private decisions and market prices do not produce an outcome that maximises relevant social value under the chosen criteria.

## Sources

Market power, externalities, public goods, common-property problems, asymmetric information, and transaction costs can create inefficiency.

## Deadweight loss

Deadweight loss represents mutually beneficial trades or gains that do not occur because of distortions, market power, or missing markets.
