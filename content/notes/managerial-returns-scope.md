## Returns to scale

Returns to scale describe output response when all inputs change together. Increasing, constant, and decreasing returns can arise from specialisation, indivisibilities, coordination, or congestion.

## Economies of scale

Economies of scale occur when long-run average cost falls as output expands. They influence plant size, outsourcing, technology, and market structure.

## Economies of scope

Economies of scope occur when producing multiple products together costs less than producing them separately because facilities, information, distribution, or brand assets are shared.
