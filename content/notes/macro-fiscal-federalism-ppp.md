## Fiscal federalism

Fiscal federalism studies how revenue powers, expenditure responsibilities, transfers, and borrowing are distributed across levels of government.

## Coordination

Clear responsibilities, transparent transfers, accountability, and local information can improve public-service delivery. Poor coordination can create duplication, unfunded mandates, or fiscal stress.

## Public–private partnerships

A public–private partnership combines public objectives with private finance or management for a defined project. Contracts must address risk sharing, service quality, affordability, and accountability.
