## Risk and uncertainty

Risk describes situations where possible outcomes and their probabilities can be identified or estimated. Uncertainty refers to situations where probabilities are less reliable or unknown.

## Expected value

Expected value is a probability-weighted average of possible outcomes. It is useful for comparison but does not fully describe how people feel about risk.

## Risk preferences

A risk-averse person may prefer a certain outcome to a gamble with the same expected value. A risk-neutral person focuses mainly on expected value, while a risk-seeking person may prefer more variability in some settings.

## Risk management

Insurance, diversification, contracts, savings, information, and safety procedures can reduce exposure. The choice depends on cost, probability, severity, and the decision-maker’s preferences.
