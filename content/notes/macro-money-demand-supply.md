## Functions of money

Money serves as a medium of exchange, unit of account, store of value, and standard for deferred payment. These functions reduce the frictions of barter.

## Money supply

Money supply includes currency and deposit-based components according to the measure used. It is influenced by central-bank operations, banking behaviour, reserve conditions, and public demand for deposits.

## Demand for money

People hold money for transactions, precautionary, and speculative motives. Income, prices, interest rates, expectations, and payment technology influence the desired balances.
