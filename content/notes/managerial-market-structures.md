## Market features

Market structure depends on the number and size of firms, product differentiation, entry and exit, information, technology, and the degree of interdependence.

## Main models

Perfect competition, monopoly, monopolistic competition, and oligopoly are models that highlight different combinations of competition and market power.

## Managerial use

Classifying a market helps a firm anticipate rivals, customer responses, entry threats, pricing freedom, and regulatory attention.
