## Types of investment

Business fixed investment adds productive equipment, residential investment adds housing, and inventory investment changes stocks of goods and materials.

## Investment demand

Investment depends on expected sales, expected profitability, financing cost, technology, capacity use, taxes, confidence, and uncertainty.

## Interest and expectations

A lower interest rate can reduce the cost of finance, but investment also depends on expected returns and risk. Strong uncertainty can discourage investment even when borrowing is cheaper.
