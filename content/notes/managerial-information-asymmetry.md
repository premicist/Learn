## Asymmetric information

Information is asymmetric when one party knows more relevant information than another. This can affect contracts, quality, screening, monitoring, and trust.

## Adverse selection

Adverse selection occurs before an agreement when hidden characteristics influence participation, such as risk type or product quality. Screening and signalling can help.

## Moral hazard

Moral hazard occurs after an agreement when hidden actions change incentives, such as reduced care after insurance or weak effort when performance is difficult to observe.

## Quick check

Classify hidden product quality before a purchase and hidden effort after a contract as adverse selection or moral hazard.
