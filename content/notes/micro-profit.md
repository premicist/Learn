## Accounting profit

Accounting profit is revenue minus recorded explicit expenses. It is useful for financial reporting but does not include every opportunity cost of owned resources.

## Economic profit

Economic profit subtracts explicit and implicit opportunity costs from revenue. Zero economic profit means the firm covers its full economic cost, including the owner’s next-best alternative.

## Profit and entrepreneurship

Profit can reward risk-bearing, innovation, organisation, information, and successful responses to changing demand. Loss provides a signal that resources may need to be reorganised or moved.

## Competition and normal return

Entry and imitation can reduce abnormal economic profit. The speed of adjustment depends on barriers, information, technology, and the ability to differentiate.
