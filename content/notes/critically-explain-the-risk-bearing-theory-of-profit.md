### Meaning

The Risk Bearing Theory of Profit was propounded by Professor Hawley in 1907. According to him, “Profit is the reward for taking risk in business.” The higher the risk in any business, the higher will be the profit; the lower the risk, the lower will be the profit. If there is no risk, there will be no profit.

### **Criticism of the Risk Bearing Theory of Profit**

The major criticisms of the theory are:

i. Profit arises not because of bearing risk (as claimed by the theory) but because of efficient management and coordination of factors by the entrepreneur.

ii. Profit is not the reward for bearing risk (as claimed by the theory) but rather the reward for avoiding and reducing risk in business by efficient entrepreneurs.

iii. There is no direct relationship between risk and profit (as claimed by the theory).

iv. Profit does not arise from all kinds of risk (as claimed by the theory). It arises only from unforeseeable risks that cannot be insured, such as changes in taste, fashion, habits of people, new discoveries, etc. It does not arise from foreseeable (insurable) risks such as theft, fire, or accidents, which are not true risks in the economic sense.
