## Model features

Perfect competition assumes many buyers and sellers, a homogeneous product, free entry and exit, good information, and no individual firm able to influence price.

## Short-run equilibrium

The firm accepts the market price and chooses output where price equals marginal revenue and marginal cost, provided the rising marginal-cost condition holds. It may earn profit, normal profit, or loss.

## Supply curve

The firm’s short-run supply curve is commonly related to the rising portion of marginal cost above average variable cost. Industry supply combines firms’ quantities at each price.

## Long-run equilibrium

Entry and exit respond to profit and loss. In the standard model, long-run adjustment tends toward normal profit, with price equal to relevant minimum average cost.
