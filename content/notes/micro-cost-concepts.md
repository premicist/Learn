## Explicit and implicit cost

Explicit costs involve direct payments, such as wages or rent. Implicit costs are the opportunity costs of resources owned by the firm, such as the owner’s time or capital.

## Accounting and economic cost

Accounting cost usually records explicit expenses. Economic cost includes explicit and implicit opportunity costs, giving a broader measure of the resources used.

## Fixed and variable cost

Fixed cost does not change with output in the short run, while variable cost changes with production. Total cost is the sum of fixed and variable cost.

## Time and cost classification

A cost can be fixed in the short run but variable in the long run. Always identify the time period and decision being analysed.
