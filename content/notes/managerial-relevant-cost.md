## Relevant cost

A relevant cost changes because of the decision being considered. Sunk costs are already incurred and should not affect a current choice, while opportunity costs can be relevant.

## Special orders

When spare capacity exists, a firm can compare incremental revenue with incremental cost, but it must consider quality, customer conflict, capacity use, and future pricing.

## Shutdown and make-or-buy

Short-run shutdown, outsourcing, and product-mix choices require comparison of avoidable costs, contribution, opportunity cost, and strategic consequences.
