## Isoquants

An isoquant shows combinations of two inputs that produce the same output. Its slope reflects the marginal rate of technical substitution between inputs.

## Isocost line

An isocost line shows combinations of inputs that can be purchased for a given total cost. Its slope depends on the relative prices of the inputs.

## Least-cost combination

For an interior solution, the firm chooses the tangency between the highest attainable isoquant and the lowest relevant isocost line. The input trade-off equals the input price ratio.

## Output and cost objectives

A firm may minimise cost for a target output or maximise output for a fixed cost. Both approaches use the same technical and price information.
