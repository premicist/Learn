  - question: "What does scarcity mean in economics?"
    options:
      - "All wants can be satisfied immediately"
      - "Resources are limited relative to wants"
      - "Prices never change"
      - "Only money is limited"
    answerIndex: 1
    explanation: "Scarcity means that available resources are limited compared with the wants and uses they could serve."
  - question: "The opportunity cost of a choice is:"
    options:
      - "The value of every alternative"
      - "The money price only"
      - "The value of the next-best alternative forgone"
      - "The total benefit of the choice"
    answerIndex: 2
    explanation: "Opportunity cost is the value of the next-best alternative given up, not every alternative or only the money price."
  - question: "Which factor of production refers primarily to human effort?"
    options:
      - "Land"
      - "Labour"
      - "Capital"
      - "Organisation"
    answerIndex: 1
    explanation: "Labour includes physical and mental human effort used in production."
