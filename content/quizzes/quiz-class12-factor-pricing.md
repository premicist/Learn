  - question: "Which reward is most directly associated with labour?"
    options:
      - "Rent"
      - "Wages"
      - "Interest"
      - "Profit"
    answerIndex: 1
    explanation: "Wages are payments for labour services, while rent, interest, and profit are associated with other factor rewards."
  - question: "Economic rent is best described as:"
    options:
      - "Every payment made for transport"
      - "The minimum payment needed to keep a factor in its current use"
      - "The part of a factor payment above the minimum needed to keep it in its current use"
      - "The total revenue of a firm"
    answerIndex: 2
    explanation: "Economic rent is the surplus above the transfer earning or minimum payment needed to retain a factor in its present use."
  - question: "Interest is generally understood as the return to:"
    options:
      - "The use of financial capital over time"
      - "The ownership of land only"
      - "The sale of final goods"
      - "A government budget deficit"
    answerIndex: 0
    explanation: "Interest is a return associated with lending or using financial capital over time, adjusted for risk and expectations."
  - question: "According to Wage Fund Theory, average wage is calculated as:"
    options:
      - "Number of workers divided by the wage fund"
      - "Wage fund divided by the number of workers"
      - "Total output divided by the wage fund"
      - "Profit divided by the price level"
    answerIndex: 1
    explanation: "The theory expresses average wage as W = WF/N, where WF is the wage fund and N is the number of workers employed."
  - question: "If the wage fund remains fixed but the number of workers increases, Wage Fund Theory predicts that average wage will:"
    options:
      - "Rise"
      - "Remain unchanged"
      - "Fall"
      - "Equal the rate of interest"
    answerIndex: 2
    explanation: "A fixed wage fund shared among more workers gives a lower average wage to each worker."
