title: "Class 11 Elasticity and Utility Check"
questions:
  - question: "If quantity demanded changes proportionally more than price, demand is:"
    options:
      - "Elastic"
      - "Inelastic"
      - "Unit elastic"
      - "Perfectly inelastic"
    answerIndex: 0
    explanation: "Demand is elastic when the absolute value of price elasticity is greater than one."
  - question: "Marginal utility is:"
    options:
      - "Total satisfaction from all units"
      - "Additional satisfaction from one more unit"
      - "The price of a good"
      - "The consumer’s total income"
    answerIndex: 1
    explanation: "Marginal utility measures the change in total utility caused by consuming one additional unit."
  - question: "A good is more likely to have elastic demand when it has:"
    options:
      - "No substitutes"
      - "Many close substitutes"
      - "No time for adjustment"
      - "A very small share of income in every case"
    answerIndex: 1
    explanation: "Close substitutes make it easier for consumers to change their purchases after a price change."
