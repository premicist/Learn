  - question: "Average revenue is equal to price when:"
    options:
      - "TR is zero"
      - "The firm sells no output"
      - "Average revenue is defined as total revenue divided by quantity"
      - "Marginal cost is constant"
    answerIndex: 2
    explanation: "Average revenue is TR divided by quantity, and total revenue is price multiplied by quantity, so average revenue equals price."
  - question: "Which cost normally falls as output increases because fixed cost is spread over more units?"
    options:
      - "Average fixed cost"
      - "Total fixed cost"
      - "Marginal cost in every case"
      - "Total variable cost"
    answerIndex: 0
    explanation: "Average fixed cost equals total fixed cost divided by output, so it normally falls as output rises in the short run."
  - question: "Marginal cost is the change in:"
    options:
      - "Total cost from one more unit of output"
      - "Fixed cost from a change in price"
      - "Average revenue from one less buyer"
      - "Total revenue from a change in wages"
    answerIndex: 0
    explanation: "Marginal cost measures the additional total cost of producing one additional unit."
