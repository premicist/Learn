questions:
  - question: "Marginal product is the:"
    options:
      - "Total output from all inputs"
      - "Output per unit of a variable input"
      - "Additional output from one more unit of a variable input"
      - "Fixed cost per unit"
    answerIndex: 2
    explanation: "Marginal product is the change in total product resulting from one additional unit of the variable input."
  - question: "When marginal product is positive but falling, total product usually:"
    options:
      - "Rises at a decreasing rate"
      - "Falls immediately"
      - "Becomes zero"
      - "Cannot be measured"
    answerIndex: 0
    explanation: "A positive marginal product still adds to total product, but a falling marginal product means total product rises more slowly."
  - question: "In the short run, total cost is equal to:"
    options:
      - "Total fixed cost minus total variable cost"
      - "Total fixed cost plus total variable cost"
      - "Average cost plus marginal cost"
      - "Price multiplied by quantity only"
    answerIndex: 1
    explanation: "Total cost combines costs that do not vary with output and costs that do vary with output."
